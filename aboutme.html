About Me

I am currently a 4th year student majoring in Game Design & Interactive Media at UC Irvine! I transferred here from Cerritos College last year, which is a community college in California, so this is technically my 2nd year here. I also expect to graduate by the end of Spring 2026! I'm also a first-generation college student, both at Cerritos College and at UCI, as neither of my parents have a bachelor's degree in the U.S.

One little known fact about me is that much of my game development knowledge originates from Happy Wheels! In fact, Happy Wheels is the reason I developed an interest in 2D level design and gameplay programming in the first place (just take a look at my project listed in Other Projects!). From there, I looked around the internet trying to see which beginner-friendly game engine I could start with when I discovered Godot.
